What I've built.

Working systems, not case studies.

Automation · n8n

Booking Enquiry Automation

Built in n8n for a surf-hire business in Bondi. An enquiry hits a webhook from the booking form, an AI model reads what the customer actually asked for, and the workflow routes it — a straightforward booking gets a priced reply drafted against the live rate card, anything unusual goes to a person rather than being guessed at.

AI · Small Business Ops

Surf Shop Intelligence Layer

The same business from the owner's side. Revenue from Stripe and cash records, bookings read back from the Google Calendar the booking form writes into, and the local surf forecast — one view, nothing re-keyed. An AI layer reads the live numbers and writes a plain-English weekly summary, guard-railed so it can only state numbers from the data.
