How I work.

Three shapes, depending on what you need. All of them start with a conversation and a clear scope before anyone commits to anything.

Embedded

A set number of days a week inside your team, owning a workstream end to end. Best when the work is ongoing and needs someone accountable rather than advising from outside.

2–3 days/week

Project

Fixed scope, fixed outcome — a platform selected and implemented, a procurement function stood up, a reporting model built and handed over. Priced on the work, not the hours.

4–16 weeks

Diagnostic

A short, sharp look at a problem you can't yet name — where the emissions or the spend actually sit, why the data can't be trusted, what to do first. Ends with a decision, not a deck.

1–2 weeks
